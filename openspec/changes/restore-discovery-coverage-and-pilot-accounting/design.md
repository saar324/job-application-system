## Search-plan design

Keep an explicit, versioned per-source `SearchPlan`: source, query terms, geography scope, sort, page cursor, per-origin budget, recency, and reason for each term. Seed terms from current profile preferences. Add normalized role-family variants, such as full stack/fullstack, backend/back-end, software engineer/developer, product engineer, applied AI/AI/ML, only when they match a configured family or a **verified submitted** application for the same profile. Count historical frequency but do not include rejected, simulated, draft, or generic `Direct application` titles. Cap terms per source and rotate through them across scan cycles so one source is not hammered. For a source with lexical search, reserve at least one broad supported probe in each comprehensive campaign and cover every configured role family within three completed cycles or record the blocking source budget. Record term, cursor, yield, skipped terms, and stop reason. Retire consistently empty terms only after checking sampled false negatives; keep periodic broad discovery to avoid overfitting history.

Official ATS feeds should fetch the configured board once per scan cycle, skip unlisted/closed roles and already handled stable IDs cheaply, then send all remaining plausible software role rows through normalization and full screening. The title prefilter may remove clearly excluded occupations but cannot require exact preferred phrasing. Provider query filters are hints. For paginated feeds, continue until ten actionable accepted rows, provider exhaustion, or a documented request/page/time ceiling. For unpaginated feeds, process the complete bounded response; if the response exceeds a safety cap, mark it partial. Use stable role keys across provider rows, listing links, application links, and receipts.

Do not change a role's `rolePriority` merely because its title was used as a search term. Score against verified profile facts and current role evidence. Audit a sampled set of hard exclusions and opportunistic rejects; preserve hard location, seniority, employment, pay floor, skill, and legal constraints. Add a separate `needs_fit_review`/`needs_destination` state for strong but uncertain roles. Give the bounded semantic reviewer a stratified sample from below-threshold and opportunistic rejects, not only the deterministic top 20. It must cite role evidence and profile evidence, may recommend review, and cannot override a verified hard exclusion. A missing profile skill is unknown unless the applicant explicitly confirmed its absence; keep automatic submission blocked and route suitable uncertain roles to review. Missing salary alone must not be represented as a known below-floor offer; whether it can pass automatic selection is a policy decision backed by shadow comparisons and quality review.

Role identity has confidence levels. A matching ATS board and role ID, or a verified canonical employer application URL, is strong enough for automatic handled filtering. Company plus title is only a possible duplicate: different openings can share both values. If strong identities differ, preserve both roles. If only weak identity matches, keep the role visible for review and prevent a second automatic final action until the employer identity is resolved. Record false-positive duplicate decisions in the quality corpus.

## Application-answer quality

Use deterministic filling for identity and verified reusable facts. For each substantive prose question, supply the model with the actual question, role requirements, employer context from the current listing and official research when needed, and a small set of verified applicant examples with provenance. Each example needs an ID, exact verified facts, source, owner approval or source verification, scope, and review date. Send only facts relevant to the current question. The current packet includes skills and links, which are too thin for claims about delivered projects or business impact. The research gate must test whether the available evidence can answer the *specific question*, not merely whether listing text is nonempty. The model returns a concise answer with claim-to-evidence links and may say evidence is insufficient. An independent claim review must match each material applicant claim to verified applicant evidence and each employer claim to current employer evidence, then check responsiveness to the question. A draft model's own evidence IDs do not establish support. If no draft/review provider is configured or the reviewer is unavailable, use explicit manual Codex/owner review; automatic submission of new prose remains held.

Optional motivation or portfolio questions may improve an application; classify them by relevance and answer only when they can add supported, specific information. Do not fill generic praise to satisfy an optional textarea. Reuse a prior approved answer only when the role, question intent, applicant example, and employer facts remain applicable; make same-employer/different-role reuse an explicit reviewed choice. Preserve legal, work-authorization, salary, and demographic holds. A full pre-final review checks all filled values and source provenance across pages, including prose quality, before automatic submission under any standing policy.

Freeze a privacy-safe evaluation corpus and the old-path outputs before further ranking or drafting changes. Start with at least 30 representative development openings/questions and build a separate holdout of at least 100 labeled cases for a percentage-based release gate. Include close-fit variants missed by exact titles, same-company/same-title distinct openings, false positives, employer-specific motivation, project evidence, optional high-value questions, stale approved answers, unsupported claims, missing profile evidence that is *unknown*, and incidental skill keywords. Use two reviewers blind to old/new labels, adjudicate disagreements, and score role fit, question relevance, company specificity, and factual support on a frozen 1–5 rubric. Compare old and new paths on the **same** cases, including previously accepted and rejected opportunities. Measure top-ten precision and suitable-role recall within bounded coverage, alongside match/hard-exclusion tests; report counts by source and failure type. Promotion requires zero critical unsupported applicant claims, legal-attestation errors, duplicate final actions, or false receipts; no degradation in paired median fit or answer-quality scores; and no more than a five percentage point decline in the share rated at least 4/5 on the holdout. With fewer than 100 holdout cases, report paired case changes and do not claim that percentage gate passed. Speed is evaluated only after this gate passes. For the live ten-role cohort, manually audit all ten role choices and all substantive prose before treating its throughput as a success metric.

## Employer destination and geographic evidence

Classify destinations as official verified, unresolved board link, redirected but unverified, inaccessible, or closed. Follow only bounded HTTPS redirects and documented public listing links; check employer role identity, current location restrictions, and application availability. A public-board `Worldwide` label is not conclusive if the employer says Asia-only. Only the official eligible role may enter the ready queue. Pending roles remain retriable with TTL/backoff rather than being permanently hidden by the known-role index. Block 403/429 and challenges, honor origin policy, and never bypass access controls.

## Coverage and timing contract

Each source records: enabled/attempted, query count, raw rows observed, deduplicated rows, prescreen rejects by reason, handled rows, scored rows, qualified rows, destination-pending rows, actionable selected rows, pages visited, known/unknown pages remaining, provider requests, stop reason, and elapsed time. Distinguish `exhausted` from `partial_cap`, `partial_timeout`, `cooldown`, `manual`, `challenge`, `rate_limited`, `parse_drift`, and `error`. A source that returns zero extractable jobs after a parse failure is `parse_drift`, not an empty market. Save sampled privacy-safe rejection reasons and query/cursor fingerprints for audit.

The campaign report separates discovery from application work. Define campaign throughput as total wall time divided by **new verified employer receipts**. Define per-application latency separately as first observed candidate to that role's verified receipt; its median and p95 are calculated across receipt-bearing roles, not by dividing campaign time. The campaign clock starts before candidate acquisition and ends at the last verified receipt or campaign stop, including retries, queue wait, approval wait, and failure recovery. Show attempted forms and queued/blocked roles separately. Report p50/p95 for completed stages and total wall time; do not extrapolate to 100 from zero receipts or preselected forms. If fewer than ten eligible roles exist within bounded, honestly reported coverage, classify the run as supply-limited; if ten queue but forms block, classify the corresponding exception stages. Quality gates in this change take precedence over speed milestones in `one-hour-verified-application-campaign`.
